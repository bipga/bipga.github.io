Evaluation

bpupga.evaluate(result, dataset, *, ks=(1, 5, 10))[source]

Score native pairs and available same-fit rankings after inference.

Pair scoring removes exact duplicate pairs and reports endpoint conflicts. Node classification additionally reports accuracy, balanced accuracy and unmatched-node scores for complete references. No decoder or refit is run. Rankings cover reference-matched queries and do not establish rejection quality. GRAMPA uses the current soft transform and also exposes its raw ranking; sparse candidates are not densified.

ks specifies positive integer ranking cutoffs. The return value is a dictionary containing pair, matchability, overlap_estimation, node_classification, validity, ranking and ranking_policy. Exact duplicate predicted pairs are deduplicated during pair scoring; repeated endpoints remain visible in the validity report.

Ranking is available from same-fit scores, costs, paired embeddings or sparse native candidates. An unavailable representation yields ranking=None and a reason. GRAMPA exposes its soft ranking and the original raw ranking.

With incomplete reference annotations, matchability, overlap and node classification are skipped rather than treating unannotated vertices as confirmed private vertices. Invalid cutoffs, indices or nonfinite outputs raise ValueError.

See Evaluation and metrics for metric definitions and interpretation.
