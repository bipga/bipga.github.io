Data and result records
=======================

AlignmentDataset
----------------

.. autoclass:: bpupga.AlignmentDataset
   :members: source_only_nodes, target_only_nodes, source_overlap_ratio, target_overlap_ratio, node_jaccard_overlap, pairs_to_original, summary

The record contains ``root``, ``name``, the two local-ID topology graphs,
``ground_truth``, the ``g1_old_to_new`` / ``g2_old_to_new`` maps, their inverse
maps, and ``metadata``. Original identifiers can be recovered with
``data.pairs_to_original(result.predicted_pairs)``.

AlignmentResult
---------------

.. autoclass:: bpupga.AlignmentResult
   :members: validate_shapes

``method``, ``predicted_pairs`` and ``runtime_sec`` identify the native output.
Optional fields include ``score_matrix``, ``cost_matrix``,
``source_embeddings``, ``target_embeddings`` and ``ranking_candidates``.
``larger_is_better`` describes continuous-score direction and ``metadata``
retains method-specific information.

Matrix dimensions must match the two graph orders. Sparse candidates use
local source and target IDs with a finite score; missing candidate pairs are
not fabricated. See :doc:`../extending` for a custom-adapter example.
